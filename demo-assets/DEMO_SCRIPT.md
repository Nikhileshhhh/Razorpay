# MoneyTrace — Demo Video Script

**Purpose of this file:** narration/dialogue for a generated demo video. Feed this
file **together with every image in `demo-assets/screenshots/`** into your video
tool (NotebookLM, Gemini, etc.) as two sources — the images are the visual
track, this file is the voiceover script, scene‑matched by filename.

**Tone:** confident, fast, a little dramatic — this is a pitch to a jury, not a
tutorial. Short sentences. Let the screenshots do the proving; the voice does
the framing.

**Target length:** ~3.5–4.5 minutes (26 scenes, ~8–12s each — adjust hold time
per scene to taste; a few scenes are marked as quick cuts).

**Suggested mood/music:** low tension build in the cold open → confident/driving
once the verification loop starts (scenes 04–10) → resolves calm and authoritative
by the audit/close (scenes 22–25). No music during scene 06 (the reversal) — let
the silence land.

## Notes for whoever assembles the video

- Scene numbers match screenshot filename prefixes exactly (`00-…` through `25-…`) — sort the folder alphabetically and it's already in the correct order.
- Scenes **16 → 17** and **19 → 20** are deliberate *before/after* pairs — a hard cut (no cross-fade) sells the contrast best.
- Scene **06** is the emotional peak of the first act — consider a beat of silence or a slow zoom rather than a quick cut.
- Scene **25** should echo scene **00**'s framing (same page) to close the loop visually as well as narratively.
- If you need to shorten the video, cut scenes 09, 10, and 24 first — everything else carries a distinct beat of the argument.
