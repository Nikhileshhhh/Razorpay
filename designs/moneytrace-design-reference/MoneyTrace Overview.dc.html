1MoneyTrace — /overview · Revenue Integrity Overview

What this page has to prove in the first ten seconds

READING ORDER

Dataset & scenario context → agent-claim truth comparison → six KPIs → operational summaries → prioritised cases. The claim-versus-retained-value panel comes before any KPI so no reader meets a large number before learning whether it survived.

FIVE ANSWERS

At risk = Unresolved exposure. Restored = Verified restored (bank-evidenced only). Prevented = Duplicate collection prevented. Reversed = the truth panel. Not verified = the outcome badge and every "not yet verified" status.

NON-NETTING

Restored, prevented, reversed and unresolved are four separate outcomes. Nothing on this page is added, subtracted or percentaged in the browser — each figure is a distinct field of the read model, printed as returned.

DATA PROVENANCE

All figures shown are representative of persisted demo responses for design purposes only. Implementation binds to GET /v1/overview and its validated read models — never hard-coded fixtures.

OPENING-STATE NOTES
Above the fold

At 1440 × 1100 the reader sees context, the full truth panel and all six KPI cards without scrolling; summaries and the case preview follow. The truth panel deliberately precedes the KPI grid.

Reversal reads three ways

Icon (reverse-arrow), label ("REVERSED — NOT VERIFIED") and a sentence explaining the correlated refund. Red is the third cue, never the first, and the number itself is a plain ₹0.00.

Four distinct emptinesses
₹0.00 INR — a measured zero No exposure — evaluated, nothing at risk No data — field absent from the dataset Unavailable — source did not respond

A dash is never used for any of them.

"How to read this page"

Disclosure, not a modal. Content: restored, prevented, reversed and unresolved are separate outcomes; they are never netted in the browser; each carries its own dataset stage and evaluation time; verification requires independent bank evidence.

REMEDIATION-STATE NOTES
What changed, and why it is allowed to say "verified"

Verified restored moves from ₹0.00 to ₹4,55,000.00 INR only because three independent conditions are all satisfied: bank evidence received, unique allocation, ERP closure recorded. Any one missing and the value stays "Awaiting bank evidence".

Acknowledgement is not verification

The Route acknowledgement stays on the untrusted side of the panel with a dashed SIMULATED-ACTION glyph and an explicit sentence that it did not cause the verification. The verification chain never lists it.

Stage labelling

Every value in this frame carries "stage: remediation 4/4" plus its own evaluation time, and the manifest hash differs from 1a — so a judge can see the two frames are two different persisted datasets, not the same numbers re-rendered.

A restored case still shows ₹0.00

CASE-2077 has ₹0.00 exposure and "Restored — bank evidenced". Zero exposure is printed as a measured zero, not as "No exposure" — the case did have exposure, and it was resolved.
